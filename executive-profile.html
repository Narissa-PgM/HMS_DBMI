Proposed strategic alignment · One Harvard / HMS BEACON / SMARTER

From HMS priorities to transparent execution

One Harvard is the proposed way of working—connect strategy, people, analytics, and systems through consensus and coordination, while preserving institutional decision rights. BEACON establishes strategic relevance. SMARTER turns that relevance into work packages, baselines, resources, release windows, evaluation, and recorded decisions.

Three clocks · One governed plan

12 monthsFirst-year operating releases

Prioritize, baseline, pilot, and deploy selected workstreams. Gate 1 establishes evidence; first-year completion is not all benefits realized.

18 monthsProposed delivery governance

Complete longer-running integrations, handoffs, and formal release checks through the proposed G0–G5 governance arc.

36 monthsEconomic and benefits horizon

Phase costs, verify sustained adoption, and evaluate outcomes; sponsor and academic due dates are separate hard constraints.
