03

SMARTER contract — a plan that can fail

Seven fields, each with its own failure test. A field fails if it is a verb, an assumption, or a name without a role behind it. Status is shown honestly: three fields are complete, three are at risk pending baselines, and one is missing a named reviewer. Open any field for its definition, evidence and failure test.

Lifecycle — six stages, six gates, 72 weeks

Beginning to end, with a gate, an accountable owner and emitted evidence at each boundary. A gate opens only when its evidence exists and its owner signs. Hover a stage to highlight it on the Gantt below; open a stage for deliverables, gate criteria and the human/AI split.

05

AI/ML predictive models — AI proposes, humans decide

Three models, three named human controls. No performance number is claimed: each card states the validation plan, the failure modes and exactly what the model may never do. Open any card for features, target, validation, drift monitoring and the override path.

3 models

06

Interactive visuals — detail in popups, not clutter

Five charts here, plus the lifecycle Gantt in section 04. Every mark is a real button: tab to it, hover or focus for a tooltip, Enter for a popup with the definition, provenance label, model, assumption, owner and next action. Charts never rely on colour alone and each has a written description.

6 charts total

07

Human-in-the-loop controls — who may decide what

This is the point of the proposal: automation with accountable humans. A decision-rights table, an escalation ladder, an override log and a short list of things the system may never do. Where model and human disagree, the human wins and the disagreement is logged.

AI proposes · people decide

08

Provenance ledger — every number carries its label

Four provenance labels are required by the protocol; this page adds CANDIDATE EVIDENCE and VERIFY so prior-institution facts and unverified placeholders cannot hide inside a real label. Counts are live from the figure register, not typed by hand. One rule governs the rest.
